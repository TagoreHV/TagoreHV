# 💫 About Me:

My interests lie in how intelligent systems perceive their environment, make decisions, and act in real-world and simulated settings.<br><br>Core Areas:<br>• Robotics Software Development (ROS 2)<br>• Autonomous Navigation (Nav2)<br>• Computer Vision & Perception (OpenCV)<br>• Robot Simulation (Gazebo)<br>• Programming with Python and C++<br><br>I actively learn through hands-on projects, simulations, and problem-solving, focusing on integrating perception, planning, and control for autonomous robotic systems.<br>I am open to internships, research opportunities, and collaborations in Robotics, Autonomous Systems, and AI-driven robotics.
